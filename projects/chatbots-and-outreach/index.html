Problem

Clients wanted assistants that answer from their own content, and a sales team was finding and contacting leads by hand.

Solution

Chatbots built on Gemini and Pinecone, in PHP and Python depending on the client's stack, and an n8n workflow that scrapes leads, stores them in Airtable, drafts messages with the OpenAI API and sends them on WhatsApp.

  4. WhatsAppSent through n8n

Answers come from the client's own documents, and lead outreach runs without manual copying.
