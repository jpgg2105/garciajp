How to find the bottleneck

Any process, from a kitchen to a hiring pipeline to a factory, finishes work at the speed of its slowest step. Improving any other step looks productive and changes nothing.

  1. 1

    Map the steps work goes through

    Write down each step and how much it can handle in an hour, a day or a week.

  2. 2

    Look for where work piles up

    Work waits in front of the slowest step. The pile is the easiest way to find it.

  3. 3

    Improving other steps changes nothing

    Here, packing gets much faster and the output stays exactly the same.

  4. 4

    Widen the bottleneck

    Add capacity where the work waits. Output rises until another step becomes the limit.

  5. 5

    Find the next one

    Once one bottleneck is fixed, another step becomes the slowest. Check again after every change.

In short

  • A step that's 100% busy while others wait is where your next hour or dollar should go.
  • Before adding capacity, make sure the bottleneck never sits idle waiting for work or redoing work that gets thrown away.
  • It works for teams, factories, support queues, sales funnels and your own week.
